Privacy Policy

Short English version for the browser extension ReturnMind. The full German version is at Datenschutzerklärung.

The extension

Everything is stored locally. All data you enter in ReturnMind or take over from an order page is kept in your browser's local extension storage on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer or to third parties. There is no account, no cloud sync and no telemetry or analytics.

What is stored: order title, order number, order date, delivery date, return date, shop, notes and the link to the order page, plus your settings.

Reading order pages. On the order pages of supported shops (currently Amazon.de, Amazon.com and Amazon.co.uk) the extension reads order number, dates, title, delivery status and return date from the page you are viewing, only inside your browser, only to offer them for import. The extension runs on those order pages only.

Export and uninstall. You can export a backup file to your device at any time; it contains no license key. Uninstalling the extension deletes its local storage.

License check (only with a license key)

ReturnMind Pro is sold through Lemon Squeezy (Stripe, Inc.). Purchase, invoice and VAT are handled there; ReturnMind receives no payment or customer data. If you enter a license key in the extension, the extension sends that key and an instance name ("ReturnMind" plus browser name plus four random characters) to the Lemon Squeezy license API to activate it, re-checks the stored key about every seven days, and deactivates it when you remove the license. Each of these requests technically transmits your IP address to Lemon Squeezy. Without a license key no request is ever made. See the Lemon Squeezy privacy policy and the Stripe privacy policy.
