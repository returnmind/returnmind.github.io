What it is

ReturnMind is a browser extension that keeps track of your online orders and reminds you before return windows and warranties end. It is not legal advice; the deadline in your shop account is the binding one.

Free and Pro

Free is free: up to 3 stored orders, reminders for at most 3 open return windows at a time, export and import. Pro unlocks unlimited orders with reminders and warranty reminders, on up to 3 browsers.
